I put AI to work on real projects: on self-hosted infrastructure, with minimal permissions and every decision
documented. Here I share how I do it and what I have learned along the way, in case it helps someone else.

## Cases

| Case | What it shows | Built with |

All repositories are MIT-licensed and documented in English and Spanish.

## How I work

- **Local first:** faces, voices and personal data stay on my own machines.
- **A person approves** what matters: cuts, publications, anything that cannot be undone.
- **Estimates are labelled** as estimates until I have measured them.
- **Every decision is written down**, with its limits.
